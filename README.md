# Social-Media-and-Billboard-Chart-Performance
Can we classify a song as a long-running hit (more than 20 weeks on the chart) or a short-lived hit (less than 20 weeks) using its Spotify Streams, YouTube Playlist reach, TikTok posts, single status, all time rank, the month of release, and the peak position on the Billboard Hot 100?

In this research, there will be an investigation into the effect of streaming and engagement metrics on different platforms on a song’s popularity. This report will investigate two research questions: (1) Can we classify a song as a long-running hit (more than 20 weeks on the chart) or a short-lived hit (less than 20 weeks) using its Spotify Streams, YouTube Playlist reach, TikTok posts, single status, all time rank, the month of release, and the peak position on the Billboard Hot 100? and (2) Can we predict a song’s All-Time Rank using YouTube Likes, TikTok Likes, Spotify Popularity Score, Weeks on Chart, Single Status, Billboard Rank, and Month of Release? Through our analysis, we hope to gain a deeper understanding of what causes a song to perform better on the chart. This knowledge will allow those within the music industry the ability to discern what contributes most to a song’s success which will assist with music marketing. Through these questions, we will determine how previous trends we identified can allow us to make predictions that are relevant in an analysis of how the modern music industry functions.
